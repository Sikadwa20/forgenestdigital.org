Before we get started

A few things
you might ask.

How much will my website cost?

The price depends on the pages, features and content involved. After our initial conversation, I’ll send a written quote. Domain, hosting, paid apps and other ongoing costs are discussed separately so you know what to budget for.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the size of the build, your content and the integrations required. We agree a realistic delivery plan before work begins and discuss any changes along the way.

Can I review it before it goes live?

Yes. You’ll be able to inspect a preview and share feedback. Review stages and revisions are agreed in the project scope.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. We can review your current site and decide whether it needs a focused improvement, a redesign or a rebuild.

Will I be able to manage the finished site?

We discuss handover and access at the start. I can explain how to manage the parts you need, or agree ongoing support if you would prefer help with updates.

Do you work with businesses outside Portugal?

Yes. I work remotely from Lisbon and can arrange calls and project reviews around a time that works for both of us.
