How It Works

How it works

A good website project doesn’t have to be complicated. Here’s what the process usually looks like:

1

Tell us about your business

We start with a quick conversation about what your business does, who your customers are, and what you want your website to achieve.

2

Get a clear recommendation and quote

Based on your needs, you’ll get a simple recommendation for the right website package, timeline, and price.

3

Content and design planning

We help organise the pages, structure, and message so your website is clear, professional, and easy for customers to understand.

4

Build and review

Your website is designed and built, then shared with you for review. You can request revisions so everything feels right before launch.

5

Launch and support

Once approved, your site goes live. If needed, support is available for updates, hosting help, and future improvements.

Simple process. Clear communication. No unnecessary tech stress.

Ask them to comment briefly on: what problem they had before, what working together was like, what they liked about the final site, and whether they’d recommend him.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

The cost depends on the size of the website and what you need included. Smaller websites cost less, while larger or more customised projects cost more. If you get in touch, I can recommend the most suitable option and give you a clear quote.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most small business websites can be completed within a reasonable timeframe depending on the project scope, content readiness, and revisions. If you need something urgent, let me know and I’ll tell you what’s possible.

I’m not good with technology — can you still help me?

Yes, absolutely. Many clients come in feeling unsure about the technical side. I keep things simple, explain everything clearly, and help with the parts that usually feel confusing.

Do you help with domain names and hosting?

Yes. I can help you choose a domain, connect it properly, and guide you through hosting setup so everything is ready to launch smoothly.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. If your current website looks outdated, feels unclear, or no longer represents your business properly, I can help improve or rebuild it.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. All websites are built to work well across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

Can I request changes before the website goes live?

Yes. Revisions are part of the process, so you’ll be able to review the site and request reasonable changes before launch.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

I mainly help small businesses, freelancers, and service-based businesses that need a clean, professional online presence.

Do you offer support after the website is launched?

Yes. Ongoing support can be provided depending on what you need, whether that’s updates, guidance, or help with hosting and maintenance.

How do I get started?

The easiest way is to send a message through the contact form or by email. Tell me a little about your business and what you need, and I’ll get back to you with the next steps.
